# Skills, by career stage

## Data analyst
Excel, SQL, Power BI and Tableau. KPI design, data lineage, dashboards and data storytelling across sales, operations, marketing, HR and manufacturing. Side build: SQL ETL into Power BI and Tableau with data-quality checks, replacing a manual reporting cycle that took two days.

## ML engineer
Python, NLP, deep learning, artificial neural networks, feature engineering, model comparison, MLflow for tracking, versioning and serving, API-based data pipelines into a CRM. Side build: an ANN on 8,000+ CarDekho listings that beat linear regression on RMSE.

## AI / LLM engineer
LLM fine-tuning with LoRA and QLoRA, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with vector databases, LangChain for orchestration and LangSmith for tracing and evaluation. The Ask Mahi assistant on his portfolio is a RAG system: LangChain, Supabase pgvector, an open-weight model served through Hugging Face, traced with LangSmith.

## Data scientist (current direction)
Statistics, experimentation, forecasting and causal questions on top of the analyst and engineering layers.

## Business and leadership
Requirements gathering, acceptance criteria and UAT, process mapping, statistical process control, leading a 10-person unit and previously a 65-person shift.
