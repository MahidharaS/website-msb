# Case studies

## Lead engine: sales was guessing who to call
Digital Maven, 2024–present. Thousands of leads from multiple sources. High-intent leads and low-probability enquiries looked alike, and CRM attributes alone missed the intent and objections inside salesperson remarks. He built an automated pipeline that brings Meta Lead Ads, website and email leads into the CRM via API, processing about 1 lakh (100,000) records without manual handling. NLP and deep learning surface conversion signals from remarks and interaction history. MLflow handles experiment tracking, versioning and serving. Scores are integrated into the sales workflow, and the lead-scoring model reaches 94.6% accuracy. Representatives now prioritise high-probability leads. Model internals are confidential; only outcomes are shared.

## KPI system: four functions, no single truth
Conflicting local spreadsheets across four business functions made reporting slow and inconsistent. He defined and documented 12+ cross-functional KPIs with agreed definitions and data lineage, then built 6+ Power BI dashboards for lead conversion, productivity and program delivery. Reporting turnaround fell by about 40%. Lesson: a single source of truth starts with a shared definition, not a new chart.

## CRM automation: follow-ups were manual and leaky
He gathered CRM automation requirements, wrote acceptance criteria and ran user acceptance testing. Manual follow-up effort fell by about 30%.

## Shop floor: idle machines, invisible lost time
Triton Valves. Real-time production dashboards plus SPC and visual management gave continuous-improvement reviews their first quantitative baseline and lifted shift output efficiency by over 10%.

## Hospital workflows
Pepino Technology. Mapped dataflows and documented 15+ user workflows across three hospital modules, prototyping analytics and supporting UAT.
