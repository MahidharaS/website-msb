# Mahidhara's career storyline

One road, seven turns. The theme: "Alone, but not lost" — data as the lantern.

## Act 1 — The engineer (2015–2019, NIE Mysore)
Mechanical engineering taught systems, constraints and tolerances. Lesson: find the mechanism, not the symptom.

## Act 2 — First contact (2019–2020, URS Cars)
Service advisor in live operations: customers, vehicles, deadlines. Lesson: the problem is whatever the customer feels it is.

## Act 3 — The pause (2020, COVID-19)
The pandemic delayed his next role by four months; he decided data would be his lantern.

## Act 4 — The shop floor (2020–2021, Triton Valves)
Ran a 65-member shift, built real-time production dashboards, applied SPC; shift output efficiency improved by over 10%. Lesson: data only matters when someone acts on it.

## Act 5 — The pivot (2022–2024, RIMS Bangalore, KPMG, Pepino)
PGDM Business Analytics & Marketing, KPMG Business Analytics L1 & L2, internship mapping dataflows and 15+ hospital workflows.

## Act 6 — The engine (2024–now, Digital Maven)
Automated lead ingestion (~1 lakh records), NLP and deep-learning lead scoring at 94.6% accuracy, 12+ shared KPIs, leads a 10-person unit supporting about ₹50 lakh in revenue.

## Act 7 — The branch (next)
Moving into data science: open questions, experiments, models that explain why. Looking for a team with hard problems.
